Eden Cheats Manager — Delete your data

Last revised: September 27, 2026

Eden Cheats Manager (EDC) is maintained by ChrisA95G. This page explains how to request deletion of information held by the developer and how to manage information stored on your device or by other services.

Request deletion from the developer

  1. Email google.bucktooth379@passmail.com with the subject EDC data deletion.
  2. Send the request from the email address you used to contact support, if possible. Say whether you want all your support correspondence deleted or identify particular messages or attachments, for example by their dates.
  3. The developer will confirm the outcome or explain any information that must be retained. If needed, you may be asked for limited information to verify that the request concerns your data.

Do not send passwords, API tokens, game files, or prod.keys.

What is deleted or retained

The developer can delete the requested support emails, the personal information in those messages (including your email address), attachments, and copies under the developer's control. EDC does not create its own user accounts or upload your local game library to the developer.

You can request deletion before the usual retention period ends. Support messages, including correspondence about a deletion request, are normally deleted within 90 days after the request is resolved. Information still needed for an ongoing dispute or a legal obligation is retained only for that purpose and deleted when that reason no longer applies. This retention rule applies to the developer's support mailbox.

Data on your device

On Android, use your device's Settings → Apps → Eden Cheats Manager → Storage → Clear storage to remove EDC's local settings, saved token, databases, and logs. Menu wording may vary by device. This resets EDC's setup and local app data.

Files installed in Eden's folder and your original game/key files are outside EDC's private storage. Remove installed code files using EDC's removal controls before clearing storage, or manage them directly in that folder. Device backups may retain separate copies; use your backup provider's controls for those.

Data held by other services

  • Cheatslips account and API records: use Cheatslips' account-deletion controls or the contact route in its privacy policy. Removing the token from EDC Settings and saving stops future authenticated fetches; it does not delete records already held by Cheatslips.
  • Game-cover server records: contact Nintendo through its privacy contact information.
  • Google Play records, GitHub data, and device backups: direct requests to the relevant provider using its privacy or account controls.

The developer cannot remotely erase your device or delete records controlled by these independent providers. If you are unsure which route applies, email the contact above for help.
