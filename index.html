Eden Cheats Manager Privacy Policy

Last revised: September 27, 2026

Eden Cheats Manager (EDC) is maintained by ChrisA95G. For support or privacy questions, contact google.bucktooth379@passmail.com.

This policy explains how EDC handles information when you scan your game library and manage cheat codes for Eden. We refer to these as "codes" below; "code files" are the files installed in Eden's folder.

Local files and settings

EDC reads game files and the prod.keys file you select to identify games and their versions. On Android, you choose documents and folders using the system file picker. The app remembers those selections and access permissions so it can use them again. When you browse, install, or remove codes, EDC accesses the code files in the Eden folder you authorize.

EDC stores settings, theme preferences, catalogs, user-created codes, and downloaded codes on your device. Saved settings include your optional Cheatslips API token and selected file or folder locations. EDC also writes local diagnostic logs, which can contain game identifiers, filenames, paths, and error details. Older logs may be replaced during log rotation.

EDC does not automatically upload your game files, prod.keys contents, user-created codes, or diagnostic logs to the developer. The app has no advertising, analytics, or automatic crash-reporting service, and does not require an EDC account.

Game cover images

When EDC displays game covers, it downloads images over HTTPS from Nintendo's image servers at img-eshop.cdn.nintendo.net. The image provider receives your IP address, the requested image URL, and standard web-request information. These requests can happen automatically when covers are displayed, even if you do not use the optional Cheatslips feature. They do not upload your game files or prod.keys contents. The image provider controls its own server records; the developer does not receive those records.

Optional online downloads

You can use the included catalog and local features without a Cheatslips token. If you enter a token and request an online fetch, EDC sends the token and the selected game's title ID to www.cheatslips.com over HTTPS. Cheatslips also receives the connection's IP address and can associate authenticated API use with your account on that service. Downloaded codes are stored on your device.

Cheatslips operates separately from EDC. Its account, service-usage, retention, and deletion practices are described in the Cheatslips privacy policy. EDC does not control information retained by that service. To avoid further requests, stop using online fetch or remove the API token from EDC Settings and save. To revoke the token or delete data held by the service, use its account controls or contact its support team.

Device backups

Depending on your device and backup settings, Android or your device provider may back up or transfer app data, including settings and locally stored content. This is managed by the operating system or backup provider. EDC does not provide its own cloud-sync service, and the developer does not receive your device backup. Use your device or backup-provider controls to manage those copies.

Google Play purchases

If you purchase EDC through Google Play, Google processes the store transaction under its own terms and privacy policy. The EDC app does not request or store payment-card details. Purchase and refund records held by Google are separate from the app data stored on your device.

Contacting support

If you email the developer, your email address, message, and any attachments are used to answer your request and investigate the issue you report. Email and forwarding providers process those messages for delivery and storage. Send only the information needed for the request; game files, prod.keys, passwords, and API tokens are not needed for routine support.

Support messages and attachments will be deleted within 90 days after the request is resolved, unless they remain necessary for an ongoing dispute or a legal obligation. In those cases, only the information needed for that purpose will be retained, and it will be deleted when the reason no longer applies. This retention period applies to the developer's support mailbox.

If you choose to open an issue on the public GitHub repository, the issue and attachments are public and handled by GitHub. Use the support email for information you do not want to post publicly.

Retention and your choices

Local settings and saved codes remain on your device until you change or delete them, clear EDC's app storage, or uninstall EDC. Local logs may be replaced during rotation. On Android, clearing app storage removes the app's stored settings and data; copies retained by device backup services may need separate removal.

Code files installed in Eden's folder and the original game/key files are outside EDC's private app storage. Clearing EDC's storage or uninstalling it does not automatically remove those files. You can remove installed code files with EDC's removal controls or manage them directly in the selected folder.

For information you have sent directly to the developer, use the contact email above to request access, correction, or deletion. Depending on the law that applies to you, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, receive a portable copy of your data, or complain to a data-protection authority. Requests about data held by Google, Cheatslips, GitHub, or a backup provider should be directed to that provider. EDC cannot remotely access or delete files on your device.

Privacy policy website

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub logs and stores your IP address for security purposes. EDC adds no analytics, advertising, or cookies to this page. GitHub's handling of website data is described in its privacy statement.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated when the app's features or data-handling practices change. The revision date at the top identifies the latest version.
